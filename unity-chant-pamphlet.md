# Unity Chant

### A mass consensus engine that turns a million voices into one answer in days.

---

## The Problem We Both Know

Community feedback is broken. Town halls hear the loudest voices. Surveys collect data but not ideas. Public comment periods check a box but don't change decisions. The tools we have take months and still leave leadership guessing what people actually want.

The result: decisions that feel imposed, residents who feel unheard, and a gap between public will and public action that erodes trust.

## How Unity Chant Works

Everyone submits their own idea — not yes or no, but their actual answer.

Then the algorithm takes over:

- **5 people** are placed in a group with **5 ideas**
- They **discuss and vote** on the strongest idea
- **5 winning ideas** go to the next round with a new group of 25 people
- This **repeats** — 125, 625, 3,125 — until one idea remains

**A thousand residents can reach consensus in four rounds. A million in nine.**

What survives isn't the most popular answer. It's the idea that held up across every round of deliberation. Every participant had a real conversation. Every voice shaped the outcome.

## Why This Matters for Olympia

Olympia already values participatory governance — it's in the culture. But the available tools haven't kept up. Unity Chant is the infrastructure that makes real public consensus possible at city scale:

- **Any phone, any browser, no app download**
- **Days instead of months**
- **Every resident deliberates in a small group where their voice matters**
- **The result is a single clear answer the city can act on**

## What a Pilot Looks Like

1. **The city picks a real question** — budget priorities, downtown planning, parks, anything you genuinely want input on
2. **Share the link** through existing channels — newsletter, website, social media, City Hall
3. **Residents join, submit ideas, deliberate, and vote** — two weeks, start to finish
4. **The result**: one idea, vetted across multiple rounds, backed by the participation of the community
5. **Zero cost to the city**
