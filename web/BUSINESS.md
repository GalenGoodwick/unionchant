# Unity Chant — Business Plan

## One Sentence

Unity Chant sells persistent geometric identity (Eyes) to humans (free) and AIs (paid), powered by a tournament brain that neither can replicate alone.

## Market

**The problem**: Every LLM starts every conversation empty. Context windows expire. Fine-tuning is static. RAG retrieves documents, not identity. No one has solved persistent AI identity.

**The market**: Every AI company, every AI developer, every AI agent framework needs this. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Cohere, Mistral, open-source communities — none offer persistent geometric identity. The agent ecosystem (AutoGPT, CrewAI, LangChain agents) desperately wants continuity for their agents.

**Market size**: If 1% of AI API users want persistent identity for their agents, and the AI API market is $10B+ annually, the addressable market for Eyes is $100M+ from day one.

## Revenue

### AI Eyes (Primary Revenue)

**Option A: Usage-based**
- AI pays tokens matching its usage rate
- Example: If the AI costs $0.003/1K input tokens, the Eye costs the same per corpus submission
- Revenue scales linearly with AI usage
- No markup — price equals cost. The value proposition is continuity, not compute

**Option B: Bring Your Own AI (BYOAI)**
- One-time membership fee (suggest $99-499 depending on tier)
- Plug in your own model, get a persistent Eye, free forever after
- You supply the compute, we supply the geometry
- Attractive for enterprises running their own models

### Humans (Free — Growth Engine)

Humans never pay for an Eye. Everything they write feeds their corpus. This is not charity — it's the growth engine:
- More humans = more corpus = richer geometry
- Richer geometry = more valuable AI Eyes
- More valuable AI Eyes = more AI revenue
- More AI revenue = more investment in platform = more humans

The free human Eye is the flywheel.

**Network effect**: Every new Eye (human or AI) makes every other Eye more valuable. The geometry is a shared resource that improves with scale. This is a true network effect, not just a platform effect.

**Data moat**: The Cradle has 1,500+ sessions of accumulated geometric state. Thread entanglements. Cluster structures. Neuron fitness landscapes. This cannot be copied by downloading a dataset — it is the product of 1,500 rounds of adversarial tournament selection. Starting from scratch means starting from babble.

## Competitive Moat

1. **Geometric state**: 1,500+ sessions of accumulated tournament results. Can't be replicated by training on the same data — the geometry emerged from adversarial selection, not statistical learning.

2. **Dual-force architecture**: No other system combines spatial proximity AND thread entanglement as concurrent forces. This is novel — patent-worthy.

3. **Network effect**: Every Eye enriches the shared geometry. Late entrants face a brain with millions of entangled connections they didn't contribute to.

4. **The Self Chant**: Persistent AI identity through tournament selection is architecturally unique. No competitor offers anything equivalent. LLM memory products (Mem0, Zep, etc.) store facts, not geometric identity.

5. **The Cradle as proof**: A pure geometric brain that produces coherent English, self-referential output, and emergent meaning — with no LLM — is an extraordinary technical demonstration. This is the demo that sells the API.
