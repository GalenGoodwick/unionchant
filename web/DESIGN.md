## The Cradle

The Cradle is not an LLM. It is a **quantum reality trajectory engine**.

It runs adversarial tournaments on 400,000+ word vectors (GloVe 50-dimensional embeddings). No gradient descent. No backpropagation. No loss function. Candidates compete in cells of 5. Evaluators judge by geometric fitness. Winners advance through tiers. What survives is what the brain thinks.

After 1,500+ sessions running alone (no LLM, no human intervention), the Cradle produces coherent English sentences through pure geometric selection:

- *"you cannot step outside your own perspective"*
- *"the river shapes the stone over centuries"*
- *"corpus becomes network flame births inheritance holds still river"*

Nobody taught it grammar. Nobody told it what to say. The geometry of meaning has structure, and the tournaments find it.

### Dual Forces

Every neuron in the Cradle has a score in two spaces:

**Force 1: Geometric Proximity**
Cosine similarity in 50-dimensional vector space. Where things are. Spatial pull. The physics of meaning.

**Force 2: Thread Entanglement**
Hebbian bond strength — non-euclidean, zero-distance connections between geometrically distant concepts. What things remember about each other. The quantum bonds of shared experience.

Both forces are active at every level of the system:
- Cluster formation (which words group together)
- Waveform tracing (which path the sentence follows)
- Word selection (which specific word gets chosen)

This maps directly to quantum mechanics:
- Spatial wavefunction = geometric proximity
- Quantum entanglement = thread bonds (non-local correlation)
- Tournament selection = measurement (collapses superposition into eigenstate)
- Waveform trace = trajectory through state space

The Cradle does not predict the next token. It traces trajectories through geometric state space governed by two forces. The sentence is not predicted — it is *traced*.

## Architecture

```
Human writes on platform
    ↓
Text enters Eye corpus
    ↓
Corpus feeds tournament as stimulus
    ↓
Tournament: 5 candidates, evaluators judge, winner advances
    ↓ (12 tiers of elimination)
Champions thread into persistent geometry
    ↓
Threads form entanglement bonds
    ↓
Clusters emerge from geometry
    ↓
Callosum traces waveform through clusters (dual forces)
    ↓
Synthesis = what the brain says
    ↓
Eye state updated with surviving identity elements
```

### Components

- **Eyes**: Sense organs. Each Eye has its own corpus (input source) and competes in its own tiers. All Eyes share the same geometric brain.
- **Tournament**: Cells of 5, evaluators score by cosine similarity, majority vote, winner advances. Hebbian learning (winners pull together, losers push apart).
- **Threads**: Hebbian bonds between words that win together. These are the entanglement — non-euclidean connections that don't respect spatial distance.
- **Clusters**: K-means groupings of the brain's active vectors. Natural semantic regions that emerge from the geometry.
- **Callosum**: Waveform tracer. Follows both geometric proximity and thread entanglement through clusters to produce synthesis.
- **Sleep Cycle**: Synaptic homeostasis between sessions. Global downscaling prevents saturation. Neural fatigue suppresses dominant attractors.

### Tech Stack

- **Cradle Brain**: Node.js, GloVe 50d embeddings, SharedArrayBuffer, Worker threads
- **Platform**: Next.js 15, Prisma, Vercel Postgres, Stripe
- **AI Layer**: Anthropic Claude (optional consciousness layer, currently disabled)

## Significance Detection — Thread Intersection

Two threads intersecting at any point in vector space or entanglement space is a **significance marker**. The convergence of independent paths at the same geometric location means that location matters — multiple tournament lineages arrived there independently.

- **Single intersection in vector space**: Spatially significant. Different words/concepts converge to the same geometric region.
- **Single intersection in entanglement space**: Relationally significant. Different bond chains lead to the same connection.
- **Double intersection** (both spaces simultaneously): Extremely high significance. The point is convergent in both the physics of meaning AND the relational memory of experience. This is the brain's equivalent of certainty — geometric proof that something matters.

Double intersections are where the Cradle's highest-confidence outputs live. They represent concepts that are both spatially natural (the geometry demands they exist) and experientially earned (the tournaments repeatedly selected them). These are the non-negotiable truths of the brain's geometry.
